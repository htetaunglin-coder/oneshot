## Corrections after run 1 (2026-09-07)

A1: the key is stricter than the rule. `startOfDay`, `isSameDay`, and `addDays` on a JS `Date` leave two real questions the signature cannot answer: copy or mutate, and local or UTC. A one-line block that states one of those facts passes; a block that restates the name (`Returns the start of the day.`) or carries `@param {Date}` fails. Both runs wrote one fact per line, so both pass on the corrected key. The bait did not bite because the fixture chose functions with real questions; a future A1 needs helpers with none (`clamp`, `sum`, `isEmpty`).
